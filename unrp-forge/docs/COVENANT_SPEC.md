# Covenant — author specification

*Stepping into his court is signing his contract.*

This is the requested fiction/design. Implementation details and Minecraft representations are documented separately in [COVENANT.md](COVENANT.md).

## Description

The user is a **Sovereign**. Their range is their **Court**, and anyone who enters it is considered to have signed the **Covenant**: the set of laws the user has written. Every law comes with a **penalty clause**. Anyone who breaks a law is in **breach**, and the penalty is collected from them on the spot. While the Court is active, a golden seal appears on the ground under the user. Everyone inside wears a glowing signature on the back of their hand, and the text of each law hangs in the air like a parchment that anyone can read.

## Resource: Collateral

Each penalty collected from a person in breach becomes Collateral that the user holds: a stat, an ability branch, a sense, a memory, a movement or a soul. The user can spend Collateral, keep it, give it to allies or trade it back.

## Legislation — offensive

- **Enact**: writes a law and its penalty clause ("No one may run. Penalty: their Speed"). The law applies at once to everyone who has signed.
- **Terms of Entry**: sets the conditions of entering the Court ("Whoever enters gives up their weapons"). They are collected from anyone who crosses the boundary.
- **Fine Print**: hides one law. It applies just like the others, but its text does not appear in the air. The user can reveal it at any moment.
- **Foreclosure**: takes all the Collateral a person in breach still owes in one go, plus interest equal to a quarter of the debt.
- **Exile**: banishes a person in repeated breach (three breaches) from the Court. They are thrown out of the range and cannot come back in until the Court closes.

## Contracts — support

- **Pact**: offers a private deal to one person ("Your fire branch for your life"). Once accepted, the deal is enforced automatically and cannot be broken by either party.
- **Endowment**: gives an ally a stat, a branch or a soul from the user's Collateral. The ally can use it as if it were their own until the Court closes or until they break a law.
- **Proxy**: binds a signed person to the user. Any wound the user takes is transferred to them.
- **Appropriation**: spends a stolen ability branch to use it themselves, with the full strength of the original owner.

## Immunity — defense

- **Sovereign Immunity**: the user is never bound by their own laws unless they choose to be.
- **Indemnity**: declares an act "covered" ("Harm done to me is void"). For a few seconds, any wound dealt to the user is struck out and becomes a breach for whoever dealt it. **From level 5.0 onward, Indemnity is no longer temporary.**
- **Escrow**: puts one of the user's own stats or branches into escrow. It cannot be stolen, sealed or copied by anyone.
- **Arbitration**: suspends an opponent's ability for as long as they are in breach. It comes back when they have paid what they owe.

## Passive — unlocked at level 5

The user sees every signature in the Court and knows at all times who is in breach, what they owe and what they still have left to give. They also keep every soul collected through a Pact, even after the Court closes. A held soul can be summoned to them from any distance.

## Range

- Low level: 10 m Court.
- Mid level: 30 m Court.
- Elite level: 75 m Court.
- High level: 150 m Court. The user can also seal a permanent Court on a building, and its laws keep applying whether they are there or not.

## Limitations before God tier

- **Low level**: two laws at a time. Penalties are limited to stats and movements. A law must be spoken out loud before it applies, and people who entered before it was spoken can read it for three seconds before it starts to count.
- **Mid level**: five laws at a time. Unlocks Pact, Fine Print and Endowment. Each Pact must offer something real in return, or it fails to bind. A collected stat comes back to its owner one hour after the Court closes.
- **Elite level**: no limit on the number of laws. Unlocks Foreclosure, Exile, Proxy and Appropriation. Penalties can take ability branches and senses. A branch taken from a higher-tier person goes back to them after ten minutes. The user can hold a maximum of five stolen branches at once.
- **High level**: penalties can take memories and souls. A soul taken through a Pact is kept permanently. Each soul held increases the size of the user's own signature: when they hold more than ten souls, they carry their own glowing signature on their hand and become bound by one law of their Court chosen at random each day, without Sovereign Immunity.

## Covenant — God tier

### Range

- 500 m Court around the user.
- **Jurisdiction**: the user can declare a whole district (Grasshill, a school, a neighbourhood) as their Court. It stays active for 24 hours, whether the user is there or not.
- **Signature by sight**: anyone who has seen the user's golden seal at least once stays signed, at any distance, for as long as the seal's mark remains on their hand: one week.

### Unlocked at God tier

- **Constitution**: one law becomes permanent. It applies to every signed person at all times, inside or outside the Court, and only the user can repeal it.
- **Retroactive Law**: a new law applies to acts committed before it was written, up to one hour back. Anyone who did the forbidden act in that hour is in breach immediately and pays the penalty.
- **Total Forfeit**: a penalty clause can take a person's whole ability. The person in breach keeps their body and their memories but has no ability at all for as long as the user holds it as Collateral.
- **Class Action**: one penalty is collected from every person in breach at the same time, whatever their number.
- **Sovereign Pact**: a Pact can be offered to someone outside the Court. The offer reaches them as a parchment that appears in their hand, and they can accept or refuse it from wherever they are.
- **Sovereign's Due**: each day, every signed person pays a tribute of the user's choosing (a little Speed, a little Recovery, one night's memory). The tribute is added to the user's Collateral.
- **Appropriation — God**: the user can wield up to ten stolen branches at once, at the full strength of their original owners.

### Passive — God tier

The user knows at all times where every signed person is, what they owe and every Pact they are bound by. They can speak to any signed person by writing on the back of that person's hand, at any distance.

### God-tier limitations

- A Retroactive Law cannot target a breach that happened outside the Court.
- The user can hold at most five Constitutions at once. Each one is engraved on their own body in gold letters, and anyone who reads it learns its full text, penalty clause included.
- The user no longer has consequences linked to the number of souls they own.
- When a person refuses a Sovereign Pact, they become immune to the user's Pacts for one hour.

**Author clarification:** at God tier the earlier tier limitations no longer apply. Indemnity is already permanent from level 5.0; permanence does not wait until God tier.
